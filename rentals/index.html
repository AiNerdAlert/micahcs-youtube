Data checklist

What we'll capture for every home.

This is the current schema. Tell me what matters that is missing and I’ll make it a first-class field.

Identity & availability 8 fields
  • Original listing URL and platform
  • Full address and map coordinates
  • Listing status: active, pending, rented, or removed
  • Date discovered and last checked
  • Property type
  • Available move-in date
  • Lease length
  • Furnished or unfurnished
Property facts 10 fields
  • Bedrooms
  • Full and half bathrooms
  • Interior square footage
  • Lot size
  • Year built
  • Number of stories
  • Parking and garage details
  • Pet policy
  • Yard and outdoor-space notes
  • Photos
True cost 12 fields
  • Base rent
  • Total monthly rent with mandatory fees
  • Effective rent after concessions
  • Security deposit
  • Application fee per adult
  • Admin and move-in fees
  • Pet deposit, pet fee, and pet rent
  • Required services and monthly add-ons
  • Utilities included
  • Utilities paid by tenant
  • Estimated cash needed to move in
  • Promotions or free-rent concessions
Included maintenance 5 fields
  • Landscaping
  • Pool and spa service
  • Pest control
  • HVAC filters or scheduled service
  • Any other landlord- or HOA-provided maintenance
Amenities & location 26+ fields
  • Pool, hot tub, fire pit, and outdoor BBQ
  • Private/fenced yard and covered patio
  • Garage, covered parking, storage, and EV charging
  • Laundry and included appliances
  • A/C, fireplace, solar, and smart-home features
  • Gated community, gym, office, or guest house
  • Neighborhood and nearby places
  • Commute, traffic, and noise notes
Your assessment 8 fields
  • One-to-five-star rating
  • High, medium, or low priority
  • Your comments
  • Highlights
  • Concerns and red flags
  • Notable details from the description
  • Deal breakers
  • After-tour verdict: yes, maybe, or no
Contact & workflow 12 fields
  • Contact name, company, phone, and email
  • Inquiry date
  • Last contact date
  • Follow-up date
  • Next action
  • Tour scheduling status
  • Scheduled tour date and time
  • Tour completion date
  • Application started and submitted dates
  • Decision date
  • Current pipeline stage
  • Closed reason if passed, unavailable, or rejected
