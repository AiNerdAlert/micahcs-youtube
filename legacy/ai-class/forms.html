Forms & Agreements Needed

Purpose: Ensure students use AI responsibly, set parent expectations, gather pre-class info

---

📋 Required Forms

1. Student Responsible Use Agreement

When: During registration (parents sign) and first day of class (students sign) Purpose: Commitment to use AI tools appropriately

2. Pre-Class Student Questionnaire

When: During registration or sent before first day Purpose: Understand student's existing AI knowledge and excitement level

3. Parent Information & Consent

When: During registration Purpose: Educate parents, get consent, set expectations

4. Post-Class Feedback Survey

When: After showcase on Friday Purpose: Gather feedback for future iterations

---

📜 Form 1: Student Responsible Use Agreement

Purpose

  • Students promise to use AI tools appropriately
  • Sets clear boundaries
  • Protects Nerding from liability
  • Educates about AI capabilities and risks

    • Tone

  • NOT scary - Don't make parents panic
  • Educational - Help them understand why this matters
  • Positive - Frame as empowering responsible use
  • Age-appropriate - Kids can understand it

    • Draft Content

    ---

    AI Summer Class - Responsible Use Agreement

    Student Name: ____________________ Parent/Guardian Name: ____________________ Date: ____________________

    What You'll Learn: In this class, you'll learn to use powerful AI tools including:

  • AI chatbots (like ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini)
  • Image generation tools
  • Voice and video creation tools
  • Code assistants and more

    • These tools are AWESOME... and powerful.

    Like any powerful tool, AI should be used responsibly. Just like you wouldn't use a hammer to break windows, you should use AI to create, learn, and help — not to harm or deceive.

    I promise to:

    ✅ Keep all my AI projects school-appropriate - No inappropriate, violent, or mean content - No trying to trick or fool people with AI

    ✅ Be honest about what AI created - I'll tell people when something was made by AI - I won't pretend AI work is 100% my own work - I won't use AI to cheat on schoolwork

    ✅ Be kind and respectful - I won't use AI to bully, harass, or hurt anyone - I won't create deepfakes or fake content about real people - I'll respect others' privacy and creative work

    ✅ Think critically - I'll remember that AI can make mistakes - I'll fact-check important information - I'll use my own judgment, not just trust AI blindly

    ✅ Use AI as a tool, not a replacement for learning - AI helps me create and learn faster - But I still need to understand what I'm doing - AI assists me; I'm still in control

    During class AND after class, I'll use AI tools responsibly.

    ---

    Student Signature: ____________________ Parent/Guardian Signature: ____________________ Date: ____________________

    ---

    Parent Section (on same form or separate page)

    Dear Parents/Guardians,

    Your child will be learning to use cutting-edge AI tools in this class. These are the same tools professionals use for work, creativity, and productivity. Our goal is to teach responsible AI literacy — empowering your child to use these tools safely and effectively.

    Why this agreement matters: AI tools are powerful and accessible. Your child may already be using them (or will soon). This class teaches them HOW to use AI responsibly, think critically about AI output, and understand AI's capabilities and limitations.

    We will NOT:

  • Expose students to inappropriate content
  • Allow unsupervised AI use
  • Encourage cheating or deception
  • Create deepfakes of real people

    • We WILL:

  • Teach prompt engineering and critical thinking
  • Discuss ethics, bias, and misinformation
  • Create school-appropriate projects
  • Emphasize AI as a tool to assist learning, not replace it

    • By signing, you acknowledge your child will use AI tools during class and agree to discuss responsible AI use at home.

    Questions? Contact us at [email/phone]

    📄 Form 3: Parent Information & Consent

    Purpose

  • Educate parents about class content
  • Set expectations
  • Get consent for AI tool use
  • Address common concerns preemptively

    • Draft Content

    AI Summer Class - Parent Information & Consent

    Student Name: ____________________ Parent/Guardian Name: ____________________ Email: ____________________ Phone: ____________________

    ---

    What This Class Covers:

    Your child will learn practical AI literacy — how to USE AI as a tool for learning, creativity, and problem-solving. This is not just theory; they'll interact with real AI systems and build working projects.

    Topics include:

  • ✅ How to prompt AI effectively (give clear instructions)
  • ✅ Understanding AI capabilities and limitations
  • ✅ Different AI models and companies (OpenAI, Google, Anthropic, etc.)
  • ✅ Multimodal AI: images, voice, video, music generation
  • ✅ Robotics and the future of AI (Tesla Optimus, Boston Dynamics, etc.)
  • ✅ AI's impact on jobs and society
  • ✅ Ethics: bias, deepfakes, misinformation, responsible use
  • ✅ Hands-on building: website or web app powered by AI
  • ✅ Friday showcase where students present their projects

    • AI Tools Students Will Use:

  • AI chatbots (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or similar)
  • Image generation tools
  • Voice/music generation tools
  • Code assistants (GitHub Copilot, Replit AI, etc.)
  • Browser-based development platforms (Replit, Glitch, etc.)

    • Safety Measures:

  • ✅ Teacher-supervised AI use at all times
  • ✅ Content filters and safety settings enabled
  • ✅ No personal information shared with AI systems
  • ✅ Responsible Use Agreement signed by students
  • ✅ Age-appropriate discussions of ethics and risks

    • Your Role:

  • Discuss AI use with your child at home
  • Review the take-home packet we provide
  • Support continued learning after class ends
  • Monitor AI use outside of class

    • Consent:

    I have read and understand the class content and AI tools that will be used.

    I give permission for my child to:

  • ☐ Use AI chatbots and generative tools during class
  • ☐ Create and deploy a web-based project
  • ☐ Have their project showcased to parents on Friday
  • ☐ Receive take-home materials about AI

    • I understand that:

  • ☐ AI tools have limitations and can make mistakes
  • ☐ Students will be taught to fact-check and think critically
  • ☐ Responsible use of AI will be emphasized throughout
  • ☐ This class aims to prepare students for an AI-integrated future

    • ---
