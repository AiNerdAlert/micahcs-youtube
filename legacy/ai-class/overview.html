AI Summer STEM Class - Project Overview

Vision

A 15-hour AI curriculum for kids (grades 5-8) where AI teaches AI — using avatars, voiceovers, and interactive media, with a human teacher facilitating physical exercises that make abstract AI concepts tangible.

Constraints

  • Duration: 15 hours total (5 days × 3 hours/day, Monday-Friday)
  • Age group: Entering 5th-8th grade (~10-13 years old)
  • Teaching model: Digital content (videos/slides) handles knowledge transfer; human teacher leads physical exercises and ensures comprehension
  • Delivery: Mini website (likely) with all digital materials
  • Output: Teacher's guide + lesson materials + physical materials list

    • Core Purpose

    Frame AI as a TOOL — not magic, not scary. A tool to use effectively.

    Goal: Give kids practical AI literacy for their future. They will live in an AI-integrated world; teach them to use it well.

    Core Topics to Cover

    1. Using AI as a Tool

  • Prompting skills - How to give good instructions
  • Context & conversation - How context windows work
  • Different models - Capabilities, strengths, weaknesses
  • AI landscape - Companies, players (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta, etc.)
  • Running AI - On your computer vs in the cloud

    • 2. Robotics & Physical AI

  • Humanoid robots - Tesla Optimus, Boston Dynamics Atlas, others
  • AI + Robots - How AI will power physical machines
  • Technical challenges - Compute, energy, materials
  • Edge vs Cloud - What runs on the robot vs remote
  • Timeline & Economics - When will families buy robots? How much? Lease/finance?
  • Current state - What works now, what's still developing

    • 3. Societal Impact & Future

  • Job displacement - What's happening now, what might happen
  • Future of work - How AI changes careers
  • Expert perspectives - What leaders are saying
  • Address concerns - Real talk about fears and possibilities

    • 4. Hands-On Building

  • Interact with AI - Direct use of models (TBD which ones)
  • Build something real - Website or web app
  • Deploy it - Make it live and usable
  • Chromebook-friendly - Must work on school computers
  • Class of 20-30 - Scalable platform needed

    • 5. Foundational Concepts

  • Context windows & tokens
  • How models are trained
  • Model intelligence differences
  • Reasoning and thinking
  • Limitations and capabilities

    • Philosophy (from Nerding)

    Digital content does the teaching → Human teacher does the facilitating.
  • Videos/slideshows deliver knowledge
  • Teacher ensures understanding
  • Teacher leads physical exercises and real-world examples
  • Physical activities make abstract digital concepts visible
