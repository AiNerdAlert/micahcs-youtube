A 5-Day AI Immersion

Learn how AI works, build real projects, and prepare for an AI-powered future. For students entering grades 5-8.

🎯 What You'll Learn

Prompting skills, multimodal AI (images, voice, video), robotics, ethics, and real-world applications. Plus: build and deploy your own AI-powered web app!

📅 Schedule

5 days, 3 hours per day (9:00 AM - 12:00 PM). Progressive curriculum from foundations to hands-on building to showcase.

🎤 Nerd Showcase

Friday finale! Students present their AI-generated reports and custom web apps to parents. See what they built!

💻 Tech Included

All computers and AI tools provided. Works on Chromebooks! Students will interact with ChatGPT, Claude, image generators, and more.
