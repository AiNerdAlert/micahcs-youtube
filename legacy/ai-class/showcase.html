Parent Showcase (Friday Final Hour)

When: Friday, last 45 minutes of class (11:15 AM - 12:00 PM) What: Nerding's signature "Nerd Showcase" where kids demonstrate what they learned

---

🎯 Goals

1. Kids show off their projects - Websites, apps, AI creations they built 2. Parents experience AI - Quick hands-on demo so parents "get it" 3. Quick parent education - Brief overview of what their kids learned 4. Impressive demonstration - Parents see their kids really understand AI 5. Take-home materials - Info packet for parents

---

📅 Structure (45 minutes)

Welcome & Overview (5 min)

  • Welcome parents
  • Quick intro: "This week your kids learned AI literacy"
  • Set expectations for what they'll see

    • Parent AI Experience (10-15 min)

  • Option A: Parents try prompting AI themselves (guided exercise)
  • Option B: Parents interact with kids' projects
  • Option C: Live demo of multimodal AI (image/music/video generation)
  • Goal: Parents understand AI is a TOOL, not magic or scary

    • Student Project Showcase (20-25 min)

  • Format TBD:
    • - Each kid presents their project? (too long with 20-30 kids) - Gallery walk (parents circulate, kids demo at stations)? - Small group presentations (3-4 kids per group)? - Live on projector (selected highlights)?

    What kids show:

  • The website/app they built
  • How it works
  • What AI features they used
  • What they learned building it

    • Wrap-Up & Take-Home (5 min)

  • Hand out parent packets
  • Explain resources for continued learning
  • Q&A if time allows
  • Invite feedback

    • ---

    📦 Take-Home Parent Packet

    Contents: 1. What We Learned - One-page summary of the week's topics 2. Resources for Kids - Safe AI tools they can use at home 3. Conversation Starters - Questions parents can ask their kids 4. Responsible AI Use - Guide for parents on monitoring/supporting 5. Further Learning - Books, websites, videos for families 6. Careers in AI - For kids interested in pursuing this field 7. Glossary - AI terms explained in plain English

    Format: Printable PDF or physical handout

    ---

    🎨 Parent AI Experience Ideas

    Idea 1: Prompt Challenge

  • Parents given a simple task ("Write a poem about pizza")
  • They write a prompt for AI
  • AI generates response
  • Discuss: How could you improve the prompt?
  • Teaches: Prompt engineering basics

    • Idea 2: Spot the AI

  • Show pairs of images/text (one human-made, one AI)
  • Parents guess which is which
  • Reveal answers & discuss
  • Teaches: AI capabilities & limitations

    • Idea 3: Interact with Student Projects

  • Parents sit with their kid
  • Kid demonstrates their project
  • Parent tries using it
  • Teaches: What their kid actually built

    • Idea 4: Multimodal Demo

  • Live demonstration of AI creating:
    • - An image from a prompt - A song with Suno - A video clip - Voice cloning
  • Teaches: AI is multimodal, not just chatbots

    🖥️ Technical Setup for Showcase

    Need:

  • Projector/screen for overview
  • Student computers (or projects accessible via URLs)
  • Parent devices? (if doing hands-on)
  • Backup demo in case internet fails
  • Printed materials ready

    • If doing gallery walk:

  • Stations set up around room
  • Kids at their computers
  • Parents rotate through

    • If doing presentations:

  • Projection setup
  • Order of presenters
  • Time limits (2-3 min per kid or group)

    🎤 Teacher Script Ideas

    Opening

    > "Welcome! This week, your kids didn't just learn ABOUT AI — they learned to USE it. AI is a tool, and this class taught them how to wield it effectively. Today, they'll show you what they built."

    Before Parent Experience

    > "Before the kids present, we want YOU to experience AI firsthand. You're going to [describe activity]. Don't worry, there are no wrong answers!"

    Introducing Student Showcase

    > "Now, the moment we've all been waiting for. Your kids have spent this week building real, working AI-powered projects. Some created websites, some built apps, some generated art and music. Let's see what they made!"

    Closing

    > "What you saw today is just the beginning. AI is a tool that will shape your kids' futures. We've given them a strong foundation, and the packet you're taking home has resources to keep learning together. Thank you for trusting us with this important education!"

    ❓ Potential Parent Questions

    Be ready to answer:

  • "Is AI safe for my kid to use?"
  • "How much does this cost to use at home?"
  • "Will AI replace my kid's future job?"
  • "How do I monitor what they're doing with AI?"
  • "What if they use it to cheat on homework?"
  • "Can AI be used for bad things?"
  • "How does this help them in school?"

    • Prepare honest, balanced answers in teacher guide

    📊 Gathering Feedback

    From parents (survey/form):

  • Did this showcase help you understand AI?
  • What surprised you most?

    • From students (verbal or written):

  • What was your favorite part of the week?
  • What was hardest?
  • Would you recommend this class to a friend?
  • What do you want to build with AI next?
